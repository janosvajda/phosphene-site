I made Phosphene for myself, because I love listening to music and I like
having something nice to look at while I do. Play a song in any app and
Phosphene draws along with it: the bass, the drums, the voice, the quiet bits
and the loud bits. There are 35 visuals to choose from, and every one of them,
every drawing and every animation, is my own work. Some are built from maths,
some are little cartoons I drew, and one keeps making up new pictures by itself.

It's free. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and your music never
leaves your Mac.

## Good to know

<div class="cards">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Your music stays yours</h3>
    <p>Phosphene only listens on your Mac. It never records anything, and it never uses your microphone or screen.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Works with your apps</h3>
    <p>It hears everything your Mac plays, or just one app if you like: Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL, Deezer, Amazon Music, VLC, Safari, Chrome or Firefox.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Light on your Mac</h3>
    <p>Most of the drawing happens on the graphics chip. On an older Mac or on battery, you can lower the quality in Settings.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Calmer if you need it</h3>
    <p>Turn on Gentler effects for softer flashes and calmer motion. It also pauses by itself when you're on a call.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Version history

### 1.1 (November 2026)

- Three new visuals: Autumn Tea, Moon Tide and Solar. That makes 35.
- Analyse Music: see which instruments are playing, the spectrum, the tempo
  and more.
- The visual list is easier to find: a Visuals tab on the left, a Visuals
  button at the top, and a Visuals menu.
- A new visual shows its name for a moment. Click it to open the list.
- Lock a visual from the list, or right-click a name to Play and Lock it.
- With no music playing, the white line gently breathes and tells you what
  to do.
- Small fixes, like listening starting again properly after a menu or a
  dialog.

### 1.0 (10 November 2026)

The first version, on the Mac App Store.

- 32 visuals that move with the music, changing by themselves on the beat
  every 20 to 40 seconds.
- Choose which visuals take turns, or lock the one you like.
- Listen to everything your Mac plays, or to just one app.
- Pauses by itself when you're on a call.
- Settings for quality, frame rate, how long each visual stays, and gentler
  effects.
- Built-in help and privacy policy that work offline.
