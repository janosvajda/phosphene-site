# Phosphene support

Phosphene is a free macOS app. It requires macOS 14.2 or newer and a Metal-capable Mac.

- Start music, open Phosphene, and allow system-audio capture when asked.
- Use **Config** to choose an app, pause listening, retry capture, or try the demo.
- Use **View → Show Visuals** (⌘B) to open and pin the visual selector. Its
  checkboxes control rotation; names preview a visual. Tab moves between controls.
- Use **Phosphene → Settings** (⌘,) for quality, timing, and gentler effects.

If visuals do not react, check the visible listening status, try **Config → Retry
Audio Capture**, and check that Phosphene is allowed under System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording → System Audio Recording
Only. A relaunch may be needed after granting permission. Some protected media may not be available to capture.

Report bugs or ask questions through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/janosvajda/phosphene-site/issues).
Include your macOS version, Mac model, app version, and reproduction steps.
Do not post private audio, recordings, or other sensitive information.

Read the [privacy policy]({{ "/privacy/" | relative_url }}) for details about local audio processing.
